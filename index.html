Shoes


Running shoes are designed with specific purposes in mind depending on how they will be used. Some focus on comfort and cushioning, making them ideal for everyday training and longer runs where reducing impact is important. Others are built for racing, using lightweight materials and advanced technology to help runners go faster and use less energy. There are also specialised track spikes designed purely for competition, which are extremely light and prioritise speed and performance over comfort.
