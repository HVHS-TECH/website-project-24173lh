Nike Vomero 18

Price - $250 NZD (JD Sports)

A cushioned running shoe designed for comfort and everyday training. It features a soft, responsive foam midsole that absorbs impact well, making it ideal for long runs and recovery days. The upper is breathable and supportive, providing a secure but comfortable fit. Overall, it is a reliable option for runners who want protection and comfort over high-speed performance.

Nike Vaporfly 4

Price - $389.99 NZD (Rebel Sport)

A high-performance racing shoe that changed distance running with its advanced design. It uses lightweight ZoomX foam combined with a carbon fibre plate to improve energy return and running efficiency. This allows runners to maintain faster speeds with less fatigue, which is why it has been used in many elite-level races and record-breaking performances. It is best suited for race day rather than daily training.

Nike Air Zoom Victory 2

Price - $~350 NZD (Nike)

A track spike built for middle-distance events such as the 800m and 1500m. It is extremely lightweight and features a responsive plate that helps maximise speed and efficiency on the track. The snug fit keeps the foot secure during fast racing, allowing athletes to perform at a high level. It is designed specifically for competition rather than comfort or everyday use.
