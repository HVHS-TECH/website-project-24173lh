About Me:

I’m someone who takes running seriously and sees it as a big part of who I am. I’m always thinking about how to get faster — how to train smarter, push harder, and improve every part of my racing. I like challenging myself with tough workouts and high expectations because I want to see what I’m actually capable of. I don’t just want to participate; I want to compete at a high level and keep improving until I reach my full potential.

I care a lot about doing things properly and holding myself to a high standard.

I also think long-term about my development as an athlete. I’m willing to work harder than most people my age and stay consistent even when training is difficult. I like pushing limits and seeing where discipline and effort can take me. For me, running isn’t just something I do; it’s something I’m building myself around, and I want to keep growing stronger, faster, and more confident as an athlete.
